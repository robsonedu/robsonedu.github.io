Hi, I'm Allen.

District Instructional Coach · 15 years in education · Arkansas

I'm Allen Robson, the District Instructional Coach for Bryant Public Schools in Arkansas. I've been an educator for 15 years. Before coaching, I taught secondary ELA in grades 7–12, everything from Accelerated Honors to co-taught inclusion and all levels in between, in Bryant and in the Little Rock School District. I've also taught Creative Writing, RTI, Critical Reading and Read 180.

I'm a former nonprofit youth volunteer turned nontraditional educator who went from the insurance industry to the classroom. My philosophy in the classroom is for students to understand themselves, the world and their place in it better than they did when they first walked into the room.

Why I built these tools

The first tools I built were timers. I'd used so many timers over the years and never found the perfect one. They had too many distracting or inappropriate ads. Many were clunky to operate or hard to see, both online and physical clocks. Most of all, many had an annoying alarm sound! So I built my own.

Every tool on RobsonEDU started the same way: a real classroom need. I use them with students and teachers, and I keep improving them based on what works and what other teachers want and need.
