Grouping

Quick Jump: Grouping Strategies | Grouping README | Grouping Tool | Fixed Grouping

Boost engagement and learning through the right grouping strategy. By intentionally grouping students, teachers can balance skills, foster collaboration, and create learning communities that match the purpose of each activity.

Strategic grouping is ideal for content learning, long-term projects, and high-stakes tasks because it supports balanced skills, targeted support, and stronger outcomes. Random grouping is quick, low-prep, and useful for community-building, equity, and adaptability. A blended approach gives teachers flexibility to match the grouping strategy to the purpose.
